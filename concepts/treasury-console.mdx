The Treasury Console is the single place where you see everything happening across Corridor's settlement network — hub balances, in-flight payouts, partner settlements, reconciliation status, and the shielded lane — with explorer links to every on-chain step. A public, read-only replay is available at [udoka-am.github.io/corridor/console](https://udoka-am.github.io/corridor/console/).

## KPIs at a glance

The top of the console shows the metrics that matter most for treasury operations:

| Metric | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| Hub balances | pathUSD available across all customer accounts on Tempo |
| In-flight | Total value locked in active sagas awaiting settlement |
| Settled via partners | Cumulative value delivered to recipients since last reset |
| Saga outcomes | Completed / compensated / manual review counts |
| Reconciliation rate | Percentage of observations matched to ledger bookings |

## Settlement network map

The network panel renders the hub-and-spoke topology with live planned routes. Each corridor shows the legs the planner has resolved for the current environment — so you can see at a glance whether a payout will go Tempo → Base → Paj or Tempo → Base → Solana → partner, and whether the bridge is using Across mainnet or the simulated testnet adapter.

## Running live flows

The **Run a flow** panel lets you trigger settlement scenarios directly from the console:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="EUR → NGN payout" icon="arrow-right">
    A standard inbound remittance. Funds leave the Tempo hub, cross the Across bridge, and arrive at the Paj partner for naira delivery.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Payout with forced partner failure" icon="circle-x">
    Injects a failure at the partner step. Watch the saga compensate in reverse order: the partner booking is reversed, the bridge is unwound, and a real on-chain Tempo refund is issued.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Importer NGN → CNY" icon="arrow-left-right">
    An outbound import payment. NGN collected from the importer flows through the hub and is paid out to a supplier in the destination currency.

  <Card title="Pay 3 suppliers privately" icon="eye-off">
    A shielded batch. Funds leave Tempo, are swapped to ZEC via NEAR Intents, and are delivered as a single Ironwood transaction requiring FROST 2-of-3 approval.

## Settlement timeline

Every saga appears in the timeline with its full step history: leg kind, status, attempt count, FX rate applied, and — for every on-chain step — a direct link to the transaction in the relevant block explorer (Tempo, Solana, or Zcash). You can trace a payment from the initial ledger reservation through every bridge hop to the final partner confirmation in one view.

## Corridor board

The corridor board shows available and held balances for each customer across each corridor. **Available** is the customer's pathUSD balance on the hub ready for new payouts; **held** is the amount reserved by sagas currently in flight.

## Pre-funding forecast

The forecast panel projects the next 7 days of outflows per customer based on recent settlement volume. It shows:

- **Daily outflow** — average daily spend per corridor
- **Runway in days** — how long the current hub balance lasts at that rate
- **7-day top-up** — the amount to add today to cover projected needs

Use this to schedule funding deposits before your balance runs low, rather than topping up reactively when a saga fails for insufficient funds.

## Venue balances

The venue balances panel breaks down every balance by where it sits:

| Venue | What you see |
|---|---|
| Tempo | Treasury vault balance in pathUSD |
| Solana | Egress and ingress wallet balances in USDC |
| Partners | Funds in transit at each ramp partner |
| Zcash | Shielded vault balance in ZEC (visible only to the vault owner) |
| Clearing accounts | pathUSD ↔ USDC and USD ↔ ZEC pairs — these net to zero across completed flows |

<Note>
Clearing accounts always net to zero once the flows that created them have settled. A non-zero clearing balance indicates an in-flight conversion.
</Note>

## Cross-chain reconciliation

The reconciliation panel shows the current match status across all three observation sources — Tempo log events, partner webhooks, and Zcash viewing-key scans — alongside the vault balance check:

- **Matched / mismatch / unbooked / unreferenced** counts with drill-down per observation
- **Vault check**: live on-chain balance vs. ledger balance, with the difference and gas booked
- **Booked gas**: every Fee Manager transfer recorded to `expense:gas`

In the live demo on Moderato: **45 / 45 matched** (Tempo 30, partners 5, Zcash 10), vault difference **0.000000 pathUSD**.

## Shielded lane

The shielded lane panel covers the full lifecycle of a Zcash Ironwood batch:

<Steps>
  <Step title="FROST vault status">
    The panel shows the 2-of-3 FROST vault configuration — which signers are the customer approver, customer finance lead, and Corridor — and the current signing threshold.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pending batches">
    Each batch shows its recipients, USD and ZEC totals, encrypted invoice references, and how many signers have approved so far.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approval workflow">
    One button per signer. Once the threshold is met (any 2 of 3), `corridor-frost` co-signs with exactly those signers and the saga resumes: the PCZT is combined, broadcast, and the batch moves to settled.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Per-batch status">
    After broadcast, the batch shows its Zcash transaction ID with an explorer link, the actual fee booked to `expense:zcash-fees`, and the reconciliation status of each output against the ledger.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Auditor disclosure export

The Disclosures panel lets you generate a scoped viewing key for an auditor:

1. Select the customer and the period (e.g., October 2026).
2. The console exports the vault's unified full viewing key for that account, along with a description of exactly what it reveals and step-by-step instructions for independent verification.
3. The auditor imports the key into a view-only wallet, syncs against the Zcash node, and can verify every shielded payout for that period against the ledger — without access to any other customer's data or any ability to spend.

<Warning>
The local console is unauthenticated by design for the demo. Action, approval, and disclosure endpoints must sit behind authentication and role-based access controls before any hosted deployment.
</Warning>

## Public replay

The live demo at [udoka-am.github.io/corridor/console](https://udoka-am.github.io/corridor/console/) is a read-only replay recorded from live testnet runs:

- Data comes from recorded JSON — no action moves real funds.
- Each flow button opens the recorded run with its actual explorer links.
- The disclosure button exports the real testnet viewing key so anyone can independently verify the shielded payouts.
- The snapshot that produces this replay **refuses to publish** if any value from `.env.local` or `.keys/` appears in the recorded data.
