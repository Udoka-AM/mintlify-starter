Corridor is stablecoin settlement and treasury infrastructure built for licensed payment companies moving money into and out of Africa. You bring the license, the KYC, and the end-customer relationships. Corridor handles the rails: a single **pathUSD balance on Tempo** funds every corridor you serve, a 32-byte **Corridor Reference** travels in the memo field of every chain it touches, and the ledger reconciles automatically on both sides of each payment.

## What Corridor Replaces

Corridor eliminates the capital traps and manual work that make African corridors the most expensive payment routes on earth. The table below shows what changes when you run on Corridor rails.

| Before Corridor | With Corridor |
|---|---|
| Fiat pre-funded in a bank account in every country | One pathUSD balance on Tempo, topped up when the forecast says so |
| T+1 to T+3 settlement through correspondent banks | 0.6 s finality on the hub; minutes end-to-end through partners |
| A new banking setup per corridor (N×N integrations) | One partner per region; every spoke reaches every other spoke (N integrations total) |
| Spreadsheets and statement matching by hand | Automatic matching on a reference carried in every chain's memo field |
| Supplier prices visible on public chains | Shielded batches on Zcash Ironwood, with a viewing key scoped to one customer and period |

## What Corridor Is Not

Understanding the boundaries matters for compliance and integration design.

- **Not a money transmitter.** You hold the FX permissions, the KYC, and the end-customer relationship. For regulated outbound flows — such as Form M for Nigerian imports — Corridor carries the compliance references in saga data and produces the reconciliation trail as audit evidence, but it does not obtain or require its own transmission licence.
- **Not a wallet or an exchange.** Your customers do not trade on Corridor. They settle and reconcile payments through it.
- **Not a new chain.** Corridor composes existing chains for what each does best: Tempo for settlement, Solana and Base for partner liquidity, and Zcash for privacy. You integrate with those chains through Corridor, not instead of them.
