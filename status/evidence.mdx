## D1 — Tempo `transferWithMemo` (29 Sep)

The first milestone confirmed that a Tempo memo transfer can be found by its indexed topic and booked exactly once.

`pnpm tempo:smoke` called `transferWithMemo` on Moderato testnet. The observer filtered logs by the indexed memo topic, found the transfer, and posted one journal entry — the ledger's uniqueness constraint on `(venue, external_ref)` blocks any duplicate.

| What was tested | Result |
|---|---|
| `transferWithMemo` on Moderato (chain 42431) | Transfer confirmed |
| Found by indexed `TransferWithMemo` memo topic | ✅ |
| Booked exactly once in the ledger | ✅ |

## D7 — FROST 2-of-3 vault (30 Sep)
