Corridor is settlement and treasury infrastructure for licensed payment service providers, remittance operators, and payout companies moving money into and out of Africa. Instead of pre-funding separate bank accounts in each country, you hold **one pathUSD balance on Tempo** and Corridor handles everything from routing and settlement to reconciliation and compliance references.

## How a payment flows

Every flow is either a **collection** into the Tempo hub or a **payout** out of it. A Nigerian importer paying a supplier in Shenzhen is a naira collection followed by a yuan payout; a diaspora remittance is a euro collection followed by a naira payout.

```
Sender (EUR, SEPA)                                 Recipient (NGN, bank / mobile money)
      │                                                             ▲
      ▼                                                             │
Customer PSP ──▶ Corridor Quote ──▶ Settlement Saga ──────────────▶ Off-ramp partner (Solana)
                      │                  │                                  │
                      ▼                  ▼                                  │
               Double-entry      Tempo transfer                             │
               ledger            (TIP-20, memo=payout_id)                  │
                      │                                                     │
                      ▼                                                     │
               Treasury console ◀─── Reconciliation engine ◀─── Partner webhooks
```

<Note>
Corridor is infrastructure, not a money transmitter. You keep your licence, your KYC, and your end-customer relationships. Corridor holds, moves, and reconciles your treasury.
</Note>
